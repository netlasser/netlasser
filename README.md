## 📈 Portfolio Case Studies for Automation & B2B Lead Generation

Use these three project concepts as polished portfolio case studies. Each one is designed to show a clear business problem, the automation workflow you built, and the measurable outcomes a client would care about.

### 1. AI-Powered B2B Lead Research & Enrichment System

**Project goal:** Help a B2B service provider find qualified prospects faster by replacing manual spreadsheet research with a repeatable lead research and enrichment workflow.

**Problem:** The client was spending hours searching LinkedIn, company websites, and directories to identify potential buyers. The process was slow, inconsistent, and produced lists with missing job titles, company details, and outreach notes.

**Solution built:**

* Created an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) framework covering industry, company size, location, revenue stage, and decision-maker roles.
* Built a lead capture spreadsheet with required fields for company name, website, contact name, role, LinkedIn URL, email status, and qualification score.
* Designed an enrichment workflow to collect company context, decision-maker details, buying signals, and personalized outreach angles.
* Added a scoring system to prioritize leads by fit, urgency, and likelihood to respond.
* Prepared the final list for CRM import and cold email sequencing.

**Tools to showcase:** Google Sheets, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, Clay, Hunter, Dropcontact, Airtable, Zapier/Make, ChatGPT.

**Portfolio deliverables:** ICP document, enriched lead database, lead scoring sheet, sample outreach notes, before-and-after workflow diagram.

**Suggested result metrics:**

* Reduced manual research time by 60–75%.
* Built a clean list of 300–500 qualified leads.
* Improved sales team prioritization with lead scoring.
* Increased personalization quality for first-touch outreach.

### 2. Cold Email Outreach Automation & Follow-Up Workflow

**Project goal:** Build a complete outbound email system that helps a B2B company contact qualified leads, personalize messaging, and automate follow-ups without losing a human tone.

**Problem:** The client had a lead list but no organized outreach process. Emails were sent manually, follow-ups were inconsistent, and there was no simple way to track replies, interested prospects, or booked calls.

**Solution built:**

* Segmented leads by industry, pain point, company size, and decision-maker role.
* Created a three-step cold email sequence: opener, value-driven follow-up, and final soft close.
* Built reusable personalization fields for company trigger, role-specific pain point, and relevant offer.
* Connected the lead database to an email sending tool and automated follow-up timing.
* Added reply tracking, status updates, and CRM handoff rules for interested leads.
* Created a weekly performance report showing sent emails, opens, replies, positive replies, and booked meetings.

**Tools to showcase:** Instantly, Smartlead, Lemlist, Apollo, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Airtable, Google Sheets, Zapier/Make, ChatGPT.

**Portfolio deliverables:** Email sequence copy, automation map, CRM pipeline stages, reporting dashboard, sample personalized email examples.

**Suggested result metrics:**

* Launched a structured campaign for 500–1,000 leads.
* Improved follow-up consistency across every prospect.
* Generated a measurable reply and meeting-booking pipeline.
* Reduced manual outreach management time by 50% or more.

### 3. CRM Lead Intake, Qualification & Sales Pipeline Automation

**Project goal:** Automate the journey from new lead capture to qualification, CRM assignment, and sales follow-up so no opportunity is missed.

**Problem:** The client was receiving leads from forms, referrals, and manual prospecting, but the data lived in different places. Sales reps had to copy information by hand, leads were not scored consistently, and follow-ups were delayed.

**Solution built:**

* Created a centralized intake workflow for website forms, spreadsheet uploads, and manual lead entry.
* Standardized required lead fields, including source, company size, budget range, urgency, service need, and contact details.
* Built qualification rules that automatically label leads as high, medium, or low priority.
* Synced qualified leads into the CRM with the correct owner, stage, tags, and next task.
* Added automated notifications for high-priority leads through Slack or email.
* Built a simple dashboard showing lead source performance, pipeline stage distribution, and follow-up completion.

**Tools to showcase:** HubSpot, Pipedrive, Airtable, Typeform, Tally, Google Forms, Slack, Zapier/Make, Notion, Looker Studio.

**Portfolio deliverables:** CRM pipeline setup, automation flow diagram, qualification rules, lead intake form, dashboard screenshots, standard operating procedure (SOP).

**Suggested result metrics:**

* Reduced lead response time from hours to minutes.
* Eliminated duplicate manual data entry.
* Improved visibility into lead quality by source.
* Created a repeatable sales operations workflow for future campaigns.
